# Bikou Logo Concept (微光 / びこう)

Meaning: 微光 = "faint light", "glimmer", "dim light" - subtle illumination in darkness

Design ideas:
- Minimal geometric symbol: a crescent or thin stroke suggesting light ray
- Subtle glow/gradient - not harsh, ethereal
- Monochrome or soft accent color
- Inspired by Japanese minimalism (wabi-sabi) - clean, understated
- Could be a horizontal brush stroke that fades, or a dot emitting faint rays

ASCII concept:
    *
   ***
  *****
   ***
    *
  (soft radial) or
  `\|/` but minimal

Or a simple glyph: 微光 character stylized - but better abstract.

Keep it minimal for DDE dock/start menu.
